# ongdalsaem Community Guidelines

Effective: [Effective date]

ongdalsaem is a warm table where people share dishes with the weather. Please help keep it comfortable for everyone.

## Please do
- Share **dishes you cooked or ate yourself**. Post other people's photos only with their permission.
- Leave acorns and kind words on other people's dishes. Respect different tastes.
- When you answer a question, share what you know, kindly.

## Please don't post
- Abuse, hate speech, discrimination, harassment or threats
- Sexual, violent or gory content
- Anything illegal or that encourages danger (for example ingredients or methods that are unsafe to eat)
- Ads, spam, repeated posts, or posts pushing people to other apps or sites
- Other people's personal information (real names, contact details, exact addresses)
- Fake accounts, or photos and text that infringe copyright

## Reporting and blocking
- Use the more menu (···) on a post, comment, message or profile to **report** or **block**.
- Blocking hides each other's posts and messages. We don't tell the other person.
- We review reports within 24 hours and never tell the other person who reported them.

## What happens if the rules are broken
Depending on how serious it is, we may hide or remove content, limit features, or suspend or delete the account. If you disagree, email app.ongdalsaem@gmail.com.
