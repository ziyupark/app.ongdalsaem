## 1. What we collect and why

| Category | What | Why |

| Sign-up · sign-in | Apple or Google account ID, email, name (if shared) | Creating your account, signing in, replying to support requests |

- Your exact coordinates are never shown to other users. Posts show only the area name at the level you choose.
- Your meal plan, fridge memo and shopping list stay on your device and are never sent to our servers.
- We don't use advertising tracking or cookies.

## 2. How long we keep it
1. We keep your information until you delete your account, then delete it without delay.
2. A deactivated account is kept for 30 days and deleted automatically if you don't sign in again.
3. Support requests are kept for 3 years after they're resolved, following Korean consumer-protection record rules.
4. Records of restrictions for breaking the rules may be kept for the minimum time needed to prevent repeat abuse.
5. Electronic files are deleted so they can't be recovered.

## 3. Sharing with third parties
We don't give your personal information to third parties, except when the law requires it (for example a lawful request from investigators).

2. Edit your profile in the app. Delete your account in Settings › Account & security › Delete account.
3. Turn off location or notifications in your device settings at any time.
4. For anything else, email app.ongdalsaem@gmail.com. We'll respond within 10 days.

## 6. Children
The Service isn't for children under 14. If we learn we've collected a child's information, we delete it right away.

## 7. How we protect it

2. Database rules let each user read and write only their own data.
3. Only the operator can access personal information.
