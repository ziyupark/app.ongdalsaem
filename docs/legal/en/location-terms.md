# ongdalsaem Location-Based Service Terms

Effective: [Effective date] · Last updated: [Effective date]

For users in Korea, the Korean version (한국어) is the official one.

## 1. About these terms
These terms explain how [Operator name] ("we") provides location-based features in ongdalsaem (the "Service"), under Korea's Act on the Protection and Use of Location Information.

- Location-based service registration: [Registration number · date]

## 2. What we use your location for
1. Showing the weather where you are and dishes that suit it
2. Showing dishes and questions from neighbours nearby
3. Finding restaurants and places nearby
4. Showing the area of your posts (neighbourhood, city or country, as you set)

## 3. How we collect it
1. Only if you allow location access, only while you're using the app, and only at approximate accuracy. We never collect location in the background.
2. If you don't agree or don't allow location, you can still use the Service by choosing a city.

## 4. How long we keep it
1. Coordinates used to look up the weather or nearby places aren't stored on our servers after the lookup.
2. Posts and questions store an area name or an area cell about 5 km across, never coordinates, and are deleted with the post or your account.
3. Records of how location information was used or provided are kept for 6 months, as the law requires.

## 5. Sharing
We don't share your location without your consent. To look up weather and places, we send approximate coordinates to OpenWeather Ltd. (UK) and Google LLC (US), which return results and don't keep the coordinates.

## 6. Your rights
1. Turn off location in your device settings or in Settings › Privacy & location at any time.
2. You can ask to see or correct the records of how your location was used.
3. If you withdraw consent, we delete the location information and records without delay.

## 7. Children
The Service isn't for children under 14, so we don't collect children's location.
