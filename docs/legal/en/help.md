# ongdalsaem Help

## Getting started
**What is ongdalsaem?** An app that suggests dishes for today's weather and lets you share what you cook, together with the weather.

**How do I sign up?** Sign in with Apple or Google and you're ready. Your username is created automatically; you can change it once every 14 days in Settings.

## Weather and location
**I can't see the weather.** Allow location, or choose a city in Settings › Privacy & location › Choose city. Check your internet connection too. Tap ↻ on the weather card to try again.

**Can others see my exact location?** No. Posts show only the area name at the level you choose (neighbourhood, city or country). Change it in Settings › Privacy & location.

## Sharing dishes
**How many photos can I add?** You can add several photos to a post and crop, rotate or adjust brightness right in the app.

**How is the weather chosen?** The weather at the time and place your first photo was taken is added automatically.

**Can I keep a post to myself?** Turn off the public switch when you share it.

## Acorns and people
**What are acorns?** A public reaction for dishes you like. You can also double-tap a photo to give one.

**Someone is bothering me.** Block or report them from the more menu (···) on their profile or post.

## Diary
**Where are my meal plan and fridge memo saved?** Only on your device. They may be lost if you delete the app or change phones.

## Your account
**I want to take a break.** Settings › Account & security › Deactivate account hides your profile and dishes for 30 days. Sign in again within 30 days to get everything back; after that the account is deleted automatically.

**I want to delete my account.** Settings › Account & security › Delete account. Deleted accounts can't be restored.

## Contact
Send a message from Settings › Help & policies › Contact support, or email app.ongdalsaem@gmail.com. We reply within 3 days.
